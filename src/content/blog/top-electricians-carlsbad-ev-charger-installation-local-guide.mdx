The core difference isn't flashy marketing. It's whether the contractor understands that EV charger installation isn't a standalone job—it's an electrical system assessment.

Most homes in Carlsbad built between 1970 and 1995 have 100-amp panels. A 100A panel can technically support a Level 2 charger if nothing else is running, but real life doesn't work that way. Your air conditioning, water heater, and kitchen circuits all draw power simultaneously. Add a 40-amp charger to that mix, and you've overloaded the system. The result: tripped breakers, potential fire risk, and an unsafe installation that no licensed electrician should sign off on.

The best contractors in Carlsbad know this immediately. They don't oversell charger-only jobs. They assess your panel, calculate your actual available capacity, and recommend upgrades when necessary—even when that means a bigger project and a longer timeline.
