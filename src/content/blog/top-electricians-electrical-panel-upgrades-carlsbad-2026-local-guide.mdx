## Understanding the Panel Upgrade Timeline in Carlsbad

Here's what to expect from start to finish:

**Week 1: Consultation & Estimate**

An electrician visits your home, assesses your current panel and load, discusses your needs (EV charger, solar, additional circuits), and provides a written estimate. This takes about an hour.

**Week 2: Permit Application**

Once you approve the estimate, the electrician submits permit paperwork to the City of Carlsbad. The city typically issues permits within 3–5 business days.

**Week 3: Installation**

The actual upgrade takes 4–8 hours, depending on your home's layout and the complexity of your electrical system. [How long does panel upgrade take in Carlsbad](/blog/how-long-does-panel-upgrade-take-carlsbad/) can vary, but most jobs are completed in a single day or two half-days.

**Week 3–4: Inspection**

The city schedules an inspection, usually within a few days of installation. The inspector verifies that the work meets code. Once approved, your upgrade is officially complete.
